# Product

## Platform

adaptive

One custom brand design shared by iOS and Android (Expo, React Native). It does not switch design language per OS.

## Users
The resort owner, alone, on their own phone. There is one account and no staff access.

## Product Purpose
Track Avila Beach PH's money: record cash in and expenses, see the current balance and this month's spending, review monthly reports, and export a month as CSV or PDF.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Data lives in Supabase. Sign-in is email and password for a single account; there is no sign-up.
- Amounts are Philippine pesos (PHP).
- Categories, payment methods, and the resort profile are edited in the Supabase dashboard, not in the app.
- Entries can be backdated to any past day; the time of day is kept from when it was recorded.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: "Avila Beach PH".
- The yellow-brown palette is binding (owner's choice).
- No logo, photography, or other brand assets exist yet.
